Investor Sentiment in Social Media

ISA 381 · Concepts in Business Programming · Final Project · Fall 2025

For this team project, we used Python to analyze investor sentiment in BitcoinTalk forum posts. We built a positive-and-negative word-list scoring approach and compared it with VADER sentiment analysis.

Using pandas, Plotly, and Matplotlib, we explored daily discussion volume, grouped posts into positive, neutral, and negative categories, and compared average sentiment scores across the two methods.

Python · pandas · VADER · Plotly · Matplotlib

Future of Work: Alternative Career Pathways

MGT 291 · Introduction to Management & Leadership · Spring 2025

Our team researched how alternative education and career pathways are reshaping access to employment. The paper examines vocational programs, boot camps, certifications, and skills-based hiring alongside traditional college degrees.

We compared research from before and after 2020, considering educational costs, career adaptability, and gaps in long-term outcome research and student support.

Havelka Clubs: Golf Course Database

ISA 345 · Database Systems and Data Warehousing · Spring 2025

Our team designed a relational database for a public golf course, supporting customer records, tee time reservations, cart assignments, and clubhouse sales.

We translated business rules into an entity-relationship model, documented a design normalized to third normal form, and used SQL to define tables, maintain records, and retrieve business information.

SQL · Relational database design · ER modeling · Normalization

Business Process Management: MU Maintenance

ISA 235 · Information Technology and the Intelligent Enterprise · Fall 2024

Our team studied Miami University’s maintenance-request process, interviewed the Director of Maintenance, and mapped the existing workflow to identify communication and scheduling problems.

We proposed a maintenance app with real-time notifications and an improved scheduling portal. The project combined current and proposed process models, app mockups, and a cost-benefit analysis of the recommended changes.

BPMN · Process analysis · Interface mockups · Cost-benefit analysis
